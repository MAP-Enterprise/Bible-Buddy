3. What to try with your kid

  1. Ask Bible Buddy about today’s Verse of the Day — see if the explanation feels age-appropriate.
  2. Try the Story of the Week (David & Goliath, Noah’s Ark, etc.) — read together, then let your kid ask follow-up questions.
  3. Have your kid ask a hard question (“Why do bad things happen?”) — we want to hear if the answer lands.
  4. Try the Verse Memory Challenge — is the difficulty right?
  5. Open the Parent Dashboard — can you see what your kid talked about?

⚠️ This is beta. Crashes happen. If something breaks, tap the pink Report an issue button anytime — it’s there on every page.

4. Privacy & safety

Bible Buddy is built for kids. Parents sign up. Parents give COPPA-compliant consent for each child. Kids never hand over names, emails, or phone numbers to the app. Chats are used only to improve the product during beta — aggregated, no third-party sharing, no ads, no data sold. You can request deletion of your family’s data at any time by emailing eeadaji@gmail.com.
